## Introduction

The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) is transforming how people with disability access support in Australia. Whether you're new to the NDIS or exploring your options, understanding the fundamentals is the first step toward building the right support for your life.

This guide covers the essentials of the NDIS: what it is, how it works, and how to get started.

---

## What Is the NDIS?

The NDIS is a social insurance scheme that provides funding and support to people with permanent and significant disability in Australia. Launched in 2016, it represents a shift from traditional welfare models to a person-centred approach where participants have greater choice and control over their supports.

**Key facts:**
- Funded through a combination of taxation and the NDIS levy
- Managed by the National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA)
- Operates in all Australian states and territories
- Covers more than 500,000 participants nationwide

---

## How the NDIS Works

### 1. **Eligibility**

To access NDIS support, you must meet two key criteria:

- **Age requirement:** Between 7 and 65 years old
- **Disability requirement:** Have a permanent and significant disability that requires substantial, ongoing support across multiple life areas

Common disabilities covered include:
- Psychosocial disabilities (relating to social, emotional, or mental functioning)
- Intellectual disabilities
- Physical disabilities
- Sensory disabilities
- Autism spectrum disorder

### 2. **The NDIS Pathway**

| Step | What Happens |
|------|-------------|
| **Access Request** | Contact the NDIA or apply online to begin your journey |
| **Assessment** | The NDIA reviews your eligibility and support needs |
| **Planning** | Work with planners to create your personalised NDIS plan |
| **Plan Approval** | Your plan is reviewed and approved for funding |
| **Support Access** | Choose registered providers to deliver your funded supports |

### 3. **Your NDIS Plan**

Once approved, you receive an NDIS plan that outlines:
- Your goals and aspirations
- Approved funding amount
- Types of support funded
- How long the plan lasts (typically 1-3 years)

### 4. **Funding Categories**

NDIS funding typically covers four main categories:

1. **Core supports** – Direct personal assistance (personal care, daily living, community participation)
2. **Capital supports** – Equipment and modifications (wheelchairs, home modifications, technology)
3. **Capacity building** – Skills development to increase independence (life skills training, education support)
4. **Psychosocial supports** – For people with disabilities relating to social, emotional, or mental functioning

## Choosing Your Support Provider

Once you have a plan, you select registered NDIS providers to deliver your supports. You have the freedom to:
- Choose providers that align with your values
- Change providers if a relationship isn't working
- Work with multiple providers across different support areas

**What to look for:**
- NDIS registration and compliance
- Relevant experience with your disability type
- Person-centred, respectful approach
- Clear communication and transparent service agreements
- Strong safeguarding practices

---

## Key NDIS Principles

The NDIS is built on several core principles that guide how support is delivered:

### Partnership & Accountability
Participants work collaboratively with providers to achieve goals. Providers are accountable for quality and safety.

### Choice & Control
You have maximum choice over who supports you and how support is delivered. This principle is at the heart of the scheme.

### Inclusion & Participation
NDIS support aims to help you participate meaningfully in community life, build relationships, and pursue your interests.

### Safeguards & Rights
The NDIS framework includes strong protections for participants, including rights to complaints handling, incident reporting, and participant advocacy.

## Getting Started: Practical Next Steps

### If you think you're eligible:

1. **Contact the NDIA** – Call 1300 800 110 or visit ndia.gov.au
2. **Gather documentation** – Have medical and support-related information ready
3. **Submit your access request** – Online or by phone
4. **Prepare for planning** – Think about your goals, challenges, and vision for the future
5. **Choose your provider** – Research registered providers in your area

### Questions to ask yourself:

- What are my main support needs?
- What goals do I want to achieve in the next 12 months?
- What's important to me in how support is delivered?
- Do I need help understanding the NDIS process?

## Common Questions

**Q: Does NDIS cover all disability-related costs?**  
A: The NDIS funds reasonable and necessary supports for participants. Some services may be covered by mainstream services (health, education) instead.

**Q: Can I change my provider?**  
A: Yes. You can change providers during your plan period if a relationship isn't working or your needs change.

**Q: What if I disagree with my plan?**  
A: You can request a plan review or appeal through the NDIA's formal review process.

**Q: Do I pay for NDIS services?**  
A: The NDIA funds approved supports. You may have participant contributions depending on your plan, but support coordination helps manage this.
